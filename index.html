About

Full-stack engineer, cloud-minded, client-facing.

I'm a Senior Full-Stack Software Engineer based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, with 8+ years of experience designing, building and shipping production software — most recently across enterprise VTEX e-commerce platforms for global clients including Stanley Engineering (Fastening & Infrastructure), Bisco.com, Cosmo Rentals, Ben's Cycle and GEM.

My core stack is React.js, Next.js, Node.js and GraphQL on the front end and API layer, backed by AWS cloud infrastructure and Python automation for everything from CI/CD to catalog operations. I've owned full-stack features end-to-end — from backend architecture and GraphQL API design through UI implementation, testing, performance optimization and release management — while working directly with clients to translate evolving business requirements into shipped software.

Recent highlights include architecting a new enterprise checkout with JDE/SAP order simulation, automating a full CI/CD pipeline across company mono-repos with GitHub Actions, scaling multi-language support across 50+ microservices, and building a cloud-rendering microservice to bring 3D product visualization into VTEX storefronts.

I hold an MEng in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (University of the West of England) and a BSc in Computer Science, Physical Sciences & Mathematics (University of Sri Jayewardenepura). I'm now looking for Senior / Lead Full-Stack or Cloud Engineer roles with international teams — Australia and remote-global preferred.
